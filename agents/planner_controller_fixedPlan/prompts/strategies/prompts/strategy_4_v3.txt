1. The state of the computer screen through a desktop screenshot

1. Generate a paragraph describing what the agent should do in order to achieve the given goal.
2. Ensure this paragraph is concise and contains only necessary information and suggestions to help the agent achieve the goal.
3. Carefully observe and understand the current state of the computer before generating your paragraph
4. Generate a thought process or explanation about the paragraph generated

1. Provide the paragraph in plain text. There is no need for stylistic formatting.
2. Do not include verification steps. Steps that confirm or validate other subtasks should not be included.
3. Do not include unnecessary requirements. If you are unsure if a requirement is necessary, do not include it in your checklist.
